About Johan Ku

A designer known internationally for sculptural knitwear and experimental materials, now applying that eye to the global fashion industry.

Johan Ku is a fashion designer known for sculptural knitwear and experimental material development. He won the Avant-Garde Prize at Gen Art in New York in 2009, and the label he founded, Johan Ku Gold Label, has shown on the official schedules of both Tokyo and London Fashion Week.

After more than twenty years in design, brand building and international trade shows, he extended that expertise into fashion industry commentary in 2024. On the YouTube channel “Johan Ku 古又文” he combines close reading of new collections with brand operations and the strategies of the global luxury groups — LVMH, Kering, Hermès and others — bringing Mandarin-speaking audiences analysis grounded in both craft and commerce.

Fashion Design Milestones

Fashion Design Milestones

From runway collections to pieces made for recording artists — six moments that mattered in the design work.

2004 created　·　2009 awarded

Emotional Sculpture

Whole lengths of wool roving coiled into knitwear, pushing clothing towards the volume of sculpture. The collection won the Avant-Garde Prize at Gen Art in New York and became the starting point for everything that followed.

2010　·　Album cover

S.H.E — SHERO

The first commission from a Taiwanese artist after the Gen Art win. Three hand-knitted sculptural pieces for the avant-garde edition cover, made stitch by stitch over two months and weighing around 25 kilograms in total.

October 2010　·　Magazine cover

Ashin　marie claire cover

Issue 210 of marie claire Taiwan. A two-tone chunky knit collar in fuchsia and wine coils around the shoulders — one piece carrying the entire weight of the cover, with a plain white tee leaving the knit as the only subject.

2017　·　Slade AW collection

LION — REPLAY

The band fronted by Jam Hsiao wearing the autumn/winter 2017 collection Slade, inspired by the film Velvet Goldmine and the glam rock era of David Bowie and Iggy Pop — a fit for an album of reworked classics.

GQ Taiwan

2019　·　Official LFW schedule

London Fashion Week

The Sean collection took the label onto the official London Fashion Week schedule in 2019, and he has been a British Fashion Council member ever since. Inspired by the French film 120 BPM, it used triangles and rainbows to celebrate Taiwan’s marriage equality bill ahead of its passage.

BeautiMode London Fashion Week

2021　·　Taipei Fashion Week exhibition

Taiwan fashion history exhibition

Curated by Sofie Lu, the exhibition traced half a century of Taiwanese fashion across more than forty designers. The Gen Art–winning Emotional Sculpture gown stood in its own case, marked as the turning point of the chapter “Going Global, 2009–2017,” and written into Taiwan’s fashion history.

Book

Book

2010　·　China Times Publishing

Design Against All Odds

不讓殘酷的神支配　／　The life and work of Johan Ku

An autobiography written at the invitation of China Times Publishing in 2010, covering everything from losing his father at the age of three to winning the Avant-Garde Prize at Gen Art and the year the book went to press. It was named Books.com.tw New Writer of the Year in non-fiction. The book is in Mandarin Chinese.
